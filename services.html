What we do

Sea and Shore Services moves cargo between the Netherlands and more than 120 countries, by sea, air, road and rail.

We are a freight forwarder and customs agent based in the Rotterdam port area. That means we book the transport, prepare the declarations and documents, arrange storage where it helps, and stay responsible for the handovers in between. You work with one contact who knows your shipment, instead of chasing a carrier, a broker and a warehouse separately.

When to call us

The moments where
a call saves money.

You do not need a finished plan to get in touch. These are the points where early contact changes the outcome.

01

You have a cargo ready date

Booking early is the cheapest way to protect a delivery date. Late bookings pay for the space that is left.

02

A container is held at customs

Call the operations line. We deal with the authority directly and tell you what it means for your date.

03

You are buying from a new country

Duties, origin rules and required documents differ more than most teams expect. Check before you order.

04

Your delivery date has moved

A later sailing or a short bonded stay often costs less than demurrage and a rushed inland move.

05

You are comparing quotes

Send us the cheaper offer and ask what it leaves out. Destination charges and free time are where quotes differ.

06

Something goes wrong out of hours

Import and export support is reachable 24/7, not only during the working week.
