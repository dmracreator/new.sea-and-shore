Privacy statement

Sea and Shore Services B.V., Westfrankelandsedijk 1, 3115 HG Schiedam, The Netherlands (KvK 50625101), is responsible for personal information collected through this website.

Information we receive

This may include your name, business contact details, company, shipment details and messages you choose to send through a quote or contact form.

Why we use it

We use information to answer your request, prepare a quotation, coordinate requested logistics services and meet legal obligations. This is based on taking steps at your request before a contract, providing a service, our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or a legal obligation where applicable. We do not sell personal information.

Who may receive it

Information is shared only with employees and service providers who need it to answer your request or coordinate a shipment. Where an international partner needs information to arrange a requested movement, we share only what is necessary.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and shipment information only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive your personal information, or object to certain processing where applicable. Contact info@sea-and-shore.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

Cookies and website tools

This website uses essential browser storage to keep the container-news carousel current for up to 24 hours. No advertising cookies are used. The site uses Google Fonts and links to external services only when you choose to open them.
